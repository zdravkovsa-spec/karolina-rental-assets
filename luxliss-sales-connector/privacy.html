Privacy Policy — Luxliss Sales Connector

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Luxliss Sales Connector (“the App”) is the internal analytics tool of the haircare brand Luxliss. It connects the brand’s own Facebook Page, Instagram professional account and advertising accounts to the brand’s own CRM. The App is used only by the brand’s staff, is not offered to the general public and has no public sign-up.

1. Who we are

Data controller: the operator of the Luxliss business portfolio on Meta (Business Portfolio ID 368495673689807). Contact: zdravkov.s.a@gmail.com.

2. What data the App accesses

The App accesses, strictly on behalf of the brand’s own accounts and with their explicit consent, the following categories of data through the official Meta APIs:

  • Facebook Page and Instagram professional account data: profile information and published content.
  • Advertising performance statistics for the brand’s own campaigns.
  • Messaging data, as described in section 3.

The App does not access contact lists, payment card data, or any special categories of personal data.

3. Messaging data

With the account owner’s consent, the App accesses conversations that people start with the business on Instagram Direct and Facebook Messenger. For each conversation the App stores only metadata: the platform-scoped sender id, timestamps, message length, the direction of the message, and — when the conversation was started from an advertisement — the identifier of that advertisement.

The App does not store the text of messages, and does not send messages to customers. This data is used solely to attribute a resulting order to the advertisement that produced it, inside the brand’s own analytics.

4. Why we process this data

A significant share of the brand’s orders arrives through Instagram Direct and Messenger after an advertisement that invites people to write. Meta reports only the number of conversations started, so the brand cannot tell which advertisement produced which sale. The App exists to answer exactly that question. There is no other purpose.

5. Sharing

Data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties. It is accessible only to the brand’s own staff.

6. Storage and retention

Operational data is stored on a server controlled by the brand, protected by access credentials, and retained for as long as the brand’s analytics require. Access tokens can be revoked at any time by the account owner in their Meta settings, which immediately terminates the App’s access.

7. Your rights

Anyone whose data the App holds may request access to, correction of, or deletion of that data by writing to the contact address above. See our Data Deletion Instructions.

8. Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date.
