Terms of Service — Luxliss Sales Connector

Last updated: 26 September 2026

1. What the App is

Luxliss Sales Connector (“the App”) is the internal analytics tool of the haircare brand Luxliss, connecting the brand’s own Facebook Page, Instagram account and customer conversations to its own CRM. It is used only by the brand’s staff, is not offered to the general public and has no public sign-up.

2. Who may use it

Only the brand’s own staff, through the brand’s own accounts connected via Meta’s own authorisation dialog.

3. What the App does not do

The App does not send messages to customers, does not store the text of customer messages, and does not sell, rent or share data with third parties.

4. Your control

Account owners may disconnect the App at any time in their Meta settings, which immediately ends its access. Data handling is described in the Privacy Policy; deletion is described in the Data Deletion Instructions.

5. Compliance

The App uses only official Meta APIs and is operated in accordance with the Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies.

6. Liability

The App is provided as is, for internal operational use. The brand is not liable for interruptions caused by changes to the Meta platforms.
