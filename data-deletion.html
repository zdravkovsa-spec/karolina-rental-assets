Data Deletion Instructions — IGPF Publisher

Last updated: 13 August 2026

IGPF Publisher ("the App") is a private internal tool used to manage advertising and social media accounts on the Meta platforms on behalf of their owners. This page explains how to remove the App's access and have any stored data deleted.

Option 1 — Revoke access yourself

  1. Open Facebook and go to Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites.
  2. Find IGPF Publisher in the list.
  3. Select it and choose Remove.

This immediately and permanently terminates the App's ability to access your data.

Option 2 — Request deletion of stored data

To have any data the App has stored about your account erased, send an email to zdravkov.s.a@gmail.com with the subject line "Data deletion request", stating the Facebook Page, Instagram account or ad account concerned.

The request will be completed within 30 days and you will receive a written confirmation once the deletion is done.

What gets deleted

All access tokens, cached account identifiers, and any advertising or content statistics the App has stored in connection with your account.
